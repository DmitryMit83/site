SIA Logu Apkope processes your personal data on the basis of:

  • performance of a contract or taking steps at your request before concluding a contract;
  • the need to comply with a legal obligation applicable to SIA Logu Apkope;
  • the legitimate interests of SIA Logu Apkope or a third party;
  • the consent you have given to process personal data for one or more specific purposes.

Personal data you provide yourself:

Personal data from third parties:

Purposes of personal data processing

SIA Logu Apkope processes personal data for the following purposes:

  • sending newsletters.

Cookies

Logu Apkope uses cookies and other technologies so that we can offer our pages safely and reliably, monitor their performance and improve your user experience, including relevant content and personalised advertising on both our own and third-party sites.

For this we use information including data about the use of the site and end devices. By clicking “I agree”, you consent to the use of cookies and other technologies to process your data, including transfer to our marketing partners (third parties).

Our partners also use cookies and other technologies for personalisation, measurement and analysis of advertising. If you do not agree, we limit ourselves to the necessary cookies and technologies. You can change your consent at any time in the settings.

Recipients of personal data

In cases established by law, SIA Logu Apkope may transfer information to third parties and use outsourcing to perform functions more efficiently (for example, for the delivery of goods).

SIA Logu Apkope does not transfer your personal data to third countries.

Your rights regarding personal data

  • Access — the right to access your personal data and obtain a copy in electronic format.
  • Rectification — the right to correct inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Erasure — the right to request deletion of personal data if it is no longer needed for the processing purpose.
  • Restriction — the right to restrict the processing of personal data in certain cases.
  • Objection — the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or marketing purposes.
  • Portability — the right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise your rights, submit a written application to the SIA Logu Apkope office at Latgales iela 130A, Riga, LV-1003, presenting an identity document.

Right to withdraw consent

If the processing of personal data is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time by sending a withdrawal to the e-mail: info@loguapkope.lv

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out while the consent was valid.

Personal data retention period

SIA Logu Apkope retains personal data as long as at least one of these criteria applies:

  • there is a legal obligation to keep the data in accordance with regulations;
  • the data subject’s consent to the relevant personal data processing is in effect;
  • SIA Logu Apkope can exercise its legitimate interests.

As a general rule, SIA Logu Apkope may keep your personal data for 10 years after the end of the business relationship, unless a different period is set by regulations. Once the retention period expires, the data is securely destroyed.

Marketing and profiling

As part of marketing activities, SIA Logu Apkope may use profiling — recording, analysing and predicting customer actions to create personalised offers.

You can always opt out of commercial communication:

  • by submitting a paper application at any SIA Logu Apkope office;
  • by sending an electronic instruction to info@loguapkope.lv;
  • by contacting SIA Logu Apkope by phone.

Logu Apkope reserves the right to refuse the right of withdrawal or to withhold compensation if the goods have been used, worn or damaged, are not in the original packaging, or the packaging is substantially damaged.

If the order was not received or was refused and is returned, at your request we will refund the money, excluding delivery costs and a 10% order-processing fee (but no less than 10.00 EUR).
