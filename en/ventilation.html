The problem

Why doesn’t a modern flat’s window “breathe”?

A modern window does not “breathe” not because something is wrong with it — on the contrary, because it is too airtight.

Before — in old wooden windows:

  • ✓ There were gaps and micro-cracks
  • ✓ Air came in naturally
  • ✓ Spontaneous ventilation occurred

In modern PVC, wooden and aluminium windows:

  • ✗ Complete airtightness
  • ✗ Multi-chamber glass units
  • ✗ Seals block the air intake

👉 As a result:

  • • No natural air exchange
  • • Humidity rises
  • • Condensation appears on the glass
  • • Mould can form
  • • The air becomes “stuffy”

It’s important to understand: the window shouldn’t “breathe” — the ventilation system should.

The solution:

  • → Regular airing
  • → Micro-ventilation (if the function exists)
  • → Installing air supply valves
  • → Setting up proper ventilation in the flat

In short: the more modern the window, the more carefully planned ventilation it needs.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about ventilation valves

Does the valve damage the window?+
All the ventilation valves we offer are certified and installed strictly in accordance with the manufacturer’s technical specification, so the installation process does not harm the window. However, please note: if the valve is removed in the future, technical holes will remain in the window frame through the full depth of the frame — this is a technologically unavoidable part of the installation. We therefore recommend assessing the installation location in advance.
Won’t it be cold in winter?+
No — an air supply valve delivers fresh air in small amounts (15–40 m³/h), which warms up quickly in the room. There is no feeling of a draught. In winter the valve can be fully closed if needed.
Does the valve help against condensation and mould?+
Yes — most condensation and mould problems arise from excess humidity and the build-up of CO₂. An air supply valve provides constant air exchange, which significantly reduces the relative humidity in rooms.
Which windows can a valve be installed on?+
Standard air supply valves can be installed on most PVC windows with a profile width from 58 mm. We also work with wooden and aluminium windows — contact us to check suitability for your specific window.
