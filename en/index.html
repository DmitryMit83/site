Question

How long does window adjustment take?

Usually 5–30 minutes per window. Several windows are handled in a single visit.

Adjusting a window usually takes 5–30 minutes per window, depending on its type and the complexity of the issue. When we service several windows in one visit, the total time is significantly lower — the technician works efficiently one after another.

Before the visit we confirm the number of windows and the nature of the problem so we can agree on a time that suits you. Working hours: Monday–Friday 9:00–18:00, Saturday by prior arrangement.

Question

Does a damaged window always have to be replaced?

Not always. Most issues — adjustment, seals, hardware — can be solved without replacement.

In most cases a window can be fully restored without replacing the frame itself. The most common problems we fix with repairs:

  • Draught or cold — seal replacement or adjustment
  • Window won’t open or closes with difficulty — adjustment or hardware replacement
  • Fogged glass units — glass unit replacement while keeping the frame
  • Damp on the frame — seal or adjustment

Buying a new window costs far more than a repair. Contact us — we will assess the condition and advise the most cost-effective solution.

Question

How do I know the seal needs replacing?

The main signs are draughts, cold from the window, visible cracks or compressed rubber.

The seal should be replaced if you notice any of these signs:

  • Draught or cold air around the window frame, even when it is closed
  • Visible cracks, hardening or deformation of the seal
  • The window frame is pulling away from the glass — gaps are visible
  • Increased noise from outside
  • Condensation on the inside of the window in winter

Seals usually last 10–15 years. Replacement is quick and relatively inexpensive, but saves significantly on heating.

Do you also replace glass units?

Yes — we replace glass units in PVC, wooden and aluminium windows, aluminium façades and skylights. Faster and cheaper than a new window.

Yes, we replace glass units in various types of structures:
