    Pleated Mosquito Nets

    Pleated mosquito nets are a modern insect-protection system for windows, doors and wide openings. The fabric folds like a concertina and slides smoothly to the side.

    In recent years we increasingly install the budget pleated option on windows and balcony doors — it is practical, convenient and cheaper than roller and door systems. The client gets a modern solution with significant budget savings.
