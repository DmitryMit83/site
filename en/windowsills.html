Questions

Frequently asked questions about windowsills

How wide should a windowsill be?+
A standard windowsill projects 20–30 cm inward from the window. The optimal width is 25–35 cm, which leaves room for flower pots or decor without blocking the radiator.
How long does installation take?+
From measurement to the finished result is usually 7–14 days. The installation itself takes 1–2 hours for one window.
Can a windowsill be replaced in an old house with thick walls?+
Yes — we work with any wall thickness. In older buildings especially, it is important to choose the right width and material to prevent condensation and heat loss.
What to do about condensation on the windowsill?+
Condensation indicates poor ventilation or insufficient window insulation. Our technician will check the seals, adjust the window and, if needed, recommend an air supply valve — this significantly reduces humidity.
