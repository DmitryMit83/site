Vertical Blinds

Vertical fabric slats — a classic, functional solution for large windows, balcony glazing and offices.

Order this service
Price: from €45

What's included

  • Vertical fabric slats with precise tilt
  • Suitable for large windows and balcony doors
  • Wide choice of fabrics — light filtering or blackout
  • Slide along the track — one-way or split (two-way)
  • Durable and easy-to-clean material
  • Installation and warranty included

Design & components of vertical blinds

Vertical blinds are made from 89 mm-wide slats with an aluminium profile and a reliable CK control mechanism. Below is a detailed description of all 28 parts and components.

Standard vertical blinds

Vertical blinds 89 mm — parts & components

In standard vertical blinds the slats (89 mm) are controlled by a CK tilt mechanism and a cord-and-weight system. The set includes the following parts:

  1. Reinforced wall bracket, 16 cm (white)
  2. Wall bracket, 10 cm
  3. Wall bracket, 7.5 cm
  4. Armstrong ceiling mount
  5. Ceiling bracket
  6. Aluminium profile, white, 34
  7. Tilt rod, aluminium, 4-spline
  8. C-clip
  9. Vinyl tube OMEGA clip, 30 mm
  10. Wall ring, 11 mm
  11. Slat cord holder
  12. Control mechanism, 34, CK
  13. Carrier (runner)
  14. Magnetic carrier
  15. Clip for sloped windows
  16. Universal cord lock (for vertical blinds)
  17. Slats, 89 mm
  18. Slat weight with chain clip, standard, 34
  19. Full control chain, metal
  20. Chain weight, metal
  21. Decorative panel (false slats)
  22. Slat mounting bracket
  23. End cap (for false slat or weight)
  24. Slat fixing clip
  25. Decorative slat (weight)
  26. Cord weight with metal
  27. Bottom chain for vertical PVC, metal
  28. Slats, 89 mm (end fixing)
