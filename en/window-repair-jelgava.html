The most common window problems we solve in Jelgava

Most window problems can be solved without full replacement — with timely repair and servicing. In Jelgava we most often encounter the following situations:

  • Draughts and cold from the window edges
  • Condensation and fogging on the glass
  • The window is hard to close or won’t lock
  • Worn or damaged hardware
  • A cracked or fogging glass unit
  • A damaged or shrunken seal
