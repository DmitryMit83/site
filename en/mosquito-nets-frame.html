    Frame Mosquito Nets

    Frame mosquito nets are the classic and most common way to protect against insects, fluff, leaves and dust. The structure is a light aluminium frame with a stretched mesh that is mounted on the outside of the window.

    Frame nets are a proven, reliable solution for most standard PVC windows. If an affordable price and quick installation without drilling the window frame matter to you, this is the classic and right choice.
